✦ CONCEPT NOTES ✦
A high-end, dramatic classic-modern design focusing on heavy, multilayered backdrop drapery.
KEY ORNAMENTS & ELEMENTS
01
BACKDROP DRAPERY THE CORE STRUCTURE
A full-coverage backdrop featuring dense, high-quality fabric with a neat, heavy drape (such as premium heavy satin, velvet, or silk-textured fabric) in warm taupe / nude / champagne tones.
02
ABUNDANT CANDLES
An extensive arrangement of candles placed along the stage floor a lot of candles, creating a warm, flickering glow across the entire stage.
03
FLORAL ARRANGEMENTS ORGANIC SPILL STYLE
Rich, dense floral arrangements predominantly in shades of white, cream, and muted ivory, arranged in an organic, overflowing spill composition.
04
CHANDELIER
Instead of identical sizes, please use a mix of large and medium-sized crystal chandeliers to create visual depth and a more dynamic, layered ceiling presence.
